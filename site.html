Terms of Service

The basics of working together, in plain language.

Last updated: September 2026

Who this is

CreativeFlows is a freelance video editing service operated by Rich Malone ("I", "me"). These terms apply whenever you hire me to edit video content, whether through the contact form on this site, email, or a direct message.

The work

Every new client starts with one free edit so you can see the quality before committing to anything. After that, pricing and turnaround are agreed on per project before work begins. Packages include a set number of revisions (as listed on the pricing section of this site) — additional rounds beyond that can be arranged for an extra fee.

Payment

Rates are quoted up front. For ongoing or bulk work, I'll send an invoice on an agreed schedule. Payment is due before final files are delivered unless we've agreed otherwise in writing (including email).

Your footage, your rights

You own your raw footage and you own the final edited video. I don't reuse, resell, or repurpose your content for anything outside the agreed project without asking first. I may ask permission to show finished work in my own portfolio — you're always free to say no.

Cancellations

Either of us can cancel before editing starts, no questions asked. If work has already begun, I'll bill only for the time already put in, at a fair, prorated rate.

Limits

I put real effort and care into every edit, but I can't guarantee how a platform's algorithm treats a video, or how it performs once it's posted. My responsibility ends at delivering the edit we agreed on.

Questions

These terms can be updated occasionally as the business grows. If anything here is unclear, just ask — richmalone11@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

What happens to your information when you reach out.

Last updated: September 2026

What I collect

When you use the contact form on this site, it opens a pre-filled email in your own email app addressed to me — your name, email, and project details go directly to my inbox. I don't run any server that stores form submissions, and this site doesn't use tracking cookies, ad pixels, or third-party analytics.

Your footage and files

If a project involves sending me raw footage or project files, those are kept private and used only to complete your edit. I don't share, sell, or hand them off to anyone else, and I delete project files once the work is finished and delivered, unless you ask me to keep them longer.

Email

If you email me directly, that message lives in my inbox like any other email. I use it only to communicate with you about your project.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time what information I have from you, or ask me to delete it. Just send a message to the email below.

Questions

This policy may be updated as the business grows. Reach out anytime at richmalone11@gmail.com with questions.
